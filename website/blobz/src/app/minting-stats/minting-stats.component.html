{{ mintAmount }} BLOBZ
Details

This is the number of BLOBZ tokens you can expect to receive after submitting a proof-of-blob transaction. The amount decreases with time, and varies with blob congestion. The lower the congestion, the more tokens you'll receive.

{{ blobCongestion }}
What is blob congestion?

This represents the demand for blob space in recent Ethereum blocks, with 1 being the lowest. The lower the congestion, the more BLOBZ tokens you'll receive. For example, if congestion is 1, you'll receive 10 times more tokens than when congestion is 10.

{{ estimatedGasFee }} ETH
How is this calculated?

This is the approximate amount of ETH needed to cover the gas fee for submitting a proof-of-blob transaction to Ethereum mainnet. The actual fee may vary based on network conditions, and the actual amount will almost always be lower than this estimate.
