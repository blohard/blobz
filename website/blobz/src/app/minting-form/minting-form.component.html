Why do you need my private key?

Browser wallet software cannot submit blob transactions, so in order for us to submit a blob transaction on your behalf, we will need the private key of an Ethereum mainnet account containing enough $ETH to cover the gas fee.

The private key is used by this service only for submitting one mainnet proof-of-blob transaction. You can always use a throwaway account here that contains only the $ETH needed to cover gas fees.

If you are uncomfortable with providing a private key here but you'd still like to mint $BLOBZ, you can download this code and run your own private instance.

$BLOBZ tokens will be delivered to this address if specified, and otherwise will be delivered to the address corresponding to the private key. Tokens are always delivered on Base.
Why does this take a few minutes?

Once your blob transaction is accepted by the Ethereum network, a message is submitted to the Base network using the standard bridge to mint the tokens. This bridging operation typically requires about 10 minutes to complete.

What's this message used for?

The message you provide here will be included in the blob that this service submits to the Ethereum network in order to mint your tokens. Ethereum guarantees that blobs will be available for download for around 18 days, but some services keep them around much longer. It's just a fun way to leave your personalized imprint on the network.
